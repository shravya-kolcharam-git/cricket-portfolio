Owner upload

Add pictures, videos and posts to your site without Claude. This page works only with your private key (token). Without it, nobody can change anything.

1. Connect

2. Add pictures or a video

Photos are shrunk to a web size and their hidden location data (GPS) is removed before upload. Videos are uploaded as they are, so check them before you share: only the video file is sent, but a clip can show places or people you may not want to publish.

3. Add a post or article link

4. What you added with this page

    Remove hides an item from the site. The file stays in your repository history.

    After a save, the site updates in about one to two minutes. Reload the site (hard refresh) to see it.
